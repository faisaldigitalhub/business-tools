# Awesome Pakistani Business Tools 🇵🇰

> A curated list of the best free and low-cost tools for a small business in Pakistan — bakery, boutique, beauty salon, restaurant, gym, electronics shop, or any home-based seller — to sell online, take payments, deliver orders, and grow without a big budget. It is not Pakistani tools only: most entries are the world's best tools (Shopify, Canva, ChatGPT, NVIDIA NIM, Stripe), with a Pakistani section wherever Pakistan has its own answer — JazzCash, Easypaisa, TCS, Leopards, Daraz, Udhaar.

[![Awesome](https://awesome.re/badge.svg)](https://github.com/sindresorhus/awesome)

**Who is this for?** A shop owner in Lahore, Karachi, Islamabad or any small town who has Instagram and WhatsApp, but no website yet. A student starting a side business. A freelancer selling to Pakistani and international customers.

**How to use it:** Start with [The 5-Step Starter Kit](#the-5-step-starter-kit). Then pick one tool from each section you need. Every link was checked live on 16 November 2026. Prices and fees change — always confirm on the official site before you pay.

**No affiliate links. No paid placements.** A tool is here because it genuinely helps a Pakistani small business.

## The 5-Step Starter Kit

If you do nothing else, do these five — in this order. They cost little or nothing to start.

1. **Google Business Profile** — so customers find you on Google Maps. Free.
2. **WhatsApp Business** — your shop's number, catalogue, auto-replies, and order chat.
3. **JazzCash or Easypaisa Business** — take payments by QR, link, or wallet. Free to open.
4. **TCS or Leopards Courier** — deliver Cash on Delivery across Pakistan.
5. **Canva** — make your posts, price lists, and menu cards yourself. Free plan available.

That is a working online business. A website comes next, in [Sell Online](#sell-online--website--store).

## Sell Online — Website & Store

Tools to put your shop on the internet — from a free one-page link to a full online store.

- [Shopify](https://www.shopify.com) — Full online store, payments, and orders in one place. Paid, free trial available.
- [WooCommerce](https://woocommerce.com) — Turn a WordPress site into a store. Free plugin; you pay for hosting.
- [WordPress](https://wordpress.com) — Website and blog, free plan available. The base for most small-business sites.
- [Wix](https://www.wix.com) — Drag-and-drop website builder, no coding. Free plan available.
- [Squarespace](https://www.squarespace.com) — Clean, premium-looking websites and stores. Paid, free trial available.
- [Daraz Seller Centre](https://sellercenter.daraz.pk) — Sell on Daraz, Pakistan's biggest marketplace. Registration is free; Daraz takes a commission per sale. 🇵🇰
- [Facebook Shops](https://www.facebook.com/business/shops) — Sell from your Facebook Page and Instagram. Free to list.
- [Google Business Profile](https://business.google.com) — Free Maps and Search listing, hours, photos, and reviews.
- [Linktree](https://linktr.ee) — One link in your bio for WhatsApp, shop, menu, and location. Free plan available.
- [Beacons](https://beacons.ai) — Link-in-bio page with a small store built in. Free plan available.

## Payments in Pakistan

Take money from customers in PKR — and from abroad where the service allows it. Check each service's current fees and limits on its own site.

- [JazzCash](https://www.jazzcash.com.pk) — Pakistan's largest mobile wallet. Business account, Raast QR, and payment gateway for websites. Free to open. 🇵🇰
- [Easypaisa](https://www.easypaisa.com.pk) — Mobile wallet and digital bank, widely used for shop payments. Free to open. 🇵🇰
- [NayaPay](https://www.nayapay.com) — Digital wallet with Visa debit card, for online payments and shopping. Free to open. 🇵🇰
- [SadaPay](https://sadapay.pk) — Digital wallet with free Mastercard debit card and free bank transfers in Pakistan. 🇵🇰
- [Raast](https://www.sbp.org.pk/psd/raast.html) — State Bank of Pakistan's instant payment system — the rails behind QR and bank-to-bank payments. 🇵🇰
- [Payoneer](https://www.payoneer.com) — Receive payments from international clients and marketplaces, withdraw to JazzCash or bank. For freelancers and exporters.
- [Wise](https://wise.com) — Send and receive money internationally at real exchange rates. For customers abroad.
- [Stripe](https://stripe.com) — Online card payments for international stores. Note: Stripe does not onboard businesses based in Pakistan — use it only if your business is registered abroad.
- [PayPal](https://www.paypal.com) — The world's best-known wallet. Note: PayPal does not offer full accounts in Pakistan — do not promise customers PayPal checkout from a Pakistani business.

## Delivery & Courier — Cash on Delivery

COD is how most Pakistani online orders are paid. These couriers offer COD, pickup, and tracking.

- [TCS](https://www.tcsexpress.com) — Pakistan's largest courier, 850+ outlets, COD for small sellers, nationwide and international. 🇵🇰
- [Leopards Courier](https://www.leopardscourier.com) — Nationwide courier with Retail COD, no minimum parcels for small sellers. 🇵🇰
- [M&P Express Logistics](https://mulphilog.com) — Courier and logistics with COD and nationwide coverage. 🇵🇰
- [PostEx](https://postex.pk) — E-commerce courier built for online sellers, COD and tracking. 🇵🇰
- [Trax / SLGTrax](https://slgtrax.com) — Courier with COD payout within 24 hours of delivery (per its own site). Formerly Trax. 🇵🇰
- [Pakistan Post](https://www.pakpost.gov.pk) — Government postal service, cheapest option for light parcels, tracking at ep.gov.pk. 🇵🇰
- [Bykea](https://www.bykea.com) — Bike delivery and ride-hailing in Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad and Rawalpindi — same-city rush delivery. 🇵🇰
- [foodpanda](https://www.foodpanda.pk) — Food and grocery delivery for restaurants and shops. 🇵🇰
- [Careem](https://www.careem.com) — Rides and same-city delivery in major Pakistani cities. 🇵🇰

## Free APIs for Shops & Developers

Free APIs to build a store, a price tool, or a small app — the same idea as [public-apis](https://github.com/public-apis/public-apis).

- [Public APIs](https://github.com/public-apis/public-apis) — A collective list of free APIs for almost everything. Free.
- [OpenWeatherMap](https://openweathermap.org/api) — Weather data for delivery and farming businesses. Free tier available.
- [ExchangeRate-API](https://www.exchangerate-api.com) — Live currency rates, including USD to PKR. Free tier available.
- [REST Countries](https://restcountries.com) — Country, currency, and language data. Free.
- [JSONPlaceholder](https://jsonplaceholder.typicode.com) — Free fake data for testing your website or app.
- [DummyJSON](https://dummyjson.com) — Fake products, carts, and users for testing a store. Free.
- [OpenStreetMap](https://www.openstreetmap.org) — Free maps — embed a map to your shop without paying Google Maps.
- [Nominatim](https://nominatim.openstreetmap.org) — Turn a shop address into map coordinates. Free.
- [Unsplash API](https://unsplash.com/developers) — Free photos in your app or website. Free tier available.
- [Pexels API](https://www.pexels.com/api/) — Free stock photos and videos in your app. Free.
- [CoinGecko API](https://www.coingecko.com/en/api) — Crypto prices, if you sell to crypto customers. Free tier available.
