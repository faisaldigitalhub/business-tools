# Contributing to Awesome Pakistani Business Tools

Thank you — this list gets better when shop owners and developers add what actually works for them.

## What belongs here

- A tool that genuinely helps a small business in Pakistan — to sell, get paid, deliver, design, market, or learn.
- The tool's official website is live.
- The tool is legal in Pakistan.

## What does not belong

- Gambling, betting, or loan-shark apps.
- Pirated or cracked software.
- Paid placements — no one can pay to be listed.
- Tools you have not tried or checked.

## How to suggest a tool

1. Open an issue with: tool name, official link, category, and one line on why it helps a Pakistani shop.
2. Or open a pull request adding one line in the right section, in this format:

```markdown
- [Tool Name](https://example.com) — One-line description. Free plan available.
```

3. One tool per pull request. Keep descriptions short and honest — say `Free`, `Free plan available`, or `Paid` only if you are sure.

Suggestions in English or Roman Urdu are both welcome.
